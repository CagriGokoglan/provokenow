The best conversations rarely happen by accident. They happen when two people put the phones away, pour something good, and ask a question neither of them has asked before. This guide gives you 150 of those questions: fifty to make you laugh, fifty to turn up the warmth, and fifty to go somewhere deeper together.

Use them on a date night, on a long drive, or as a five-minute ritual before bed. There are no right answers, only honest ones.

## How to use these questions

- **Take turns, and both answer.** A question asked of only one person becomes an interview. Answer it yourself too.
- **Follow the thread.** If an answer surprises you, stay there. "Tell me more about that" is the best follow-up question ever written.
- **Pass freely.** Either of you can skip any question, any time, with no explanation. Consent is what makes honesty feel safe.
- **Go in order of intensity.** Start with the fun questions, let the flirty ones warm the room, and save the deep questions for when you both have time and attention.
- **Make it a ritual.** One evening a week is enough. Small, regular moments of curiosity build more emotional intimacy than one grand gesture a year.
